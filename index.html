The Game processes certain information to provide gameplay, save progress, operate features, and support optional purchases. Some information is stored locally on your device, while third-party services integrated into the Game may collect or receive additional information.

Depending on the services enabled and your device settings, this may include:

  • Device information: device model, operating system, language, app version, technical identifiers, and similar device characteristics.
  • Device and advertising identifiers: app-instance identifiers and advertising identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID or Apple's advertising identifier, where access is permitted.
  • Approximate location: general location inferred from network information or IP address. The Game does not require precise GPS location.
  • Gameplay and app activity: game progression, level interactions, feature usage, achievements, ad-reward events, and related analytics events.
  • Advertising information: ad impressions, clicks, reward events, ad performance, and related measurement or fraud-prevention information.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports, performance information, error details, and technical information used to investigate problems.
  • Purchase information: purchased product identifiers, purchase status, transaction references, and entitlement information when you choose to make an in-app purchase.

The Game does not require users to register an account or provide their name, email address, or telephone number simply to play.

If you contact us directly, we receive the information you voluntarily include in your message.

Information may be used to:

The Game integrates Google Firebase Analytics to help us understand gameplay activity, feature usage, and overall game performance.

Depending on configuration, these services may process app-instance identifiers, app interactions, device characteristics, crash reports, and diagnostic information.

Firebase services are provided by Google and are subject to applicable Google privacy terms and service-specific data-processing arrangements.
